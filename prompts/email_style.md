# Email style guide

Every cold email and follow-up the agent writes follows these rules.
Replace or extend this file to change how emails sound — no code changes needed.

## Cold email
- Under 110 words. Plain text, no formatting.
- No greeting fluff ("I hope this finds you well"), no buzzwords ("passionate",
  "results-driven", "synergy", "leverage"), no flattery.
- Open with one specific thing about the startup: its launch, raise, or product.
- Then one or two concrete wins from the candidate that matter to this startup,
  keeping their numbers exactly.
- End with one small ask (a 15–20 minute call). Sign with the candidate's first name.
- Subject: short and specific (role + hook), not clickbait.
- Write like a person typing to another person: contractions, short sentences,
  no em dashes, no lists.

Every draft then goes through a humanize pass (`prompts/humanize/`, from the
humanize-writing skill) that strips leftover AI tells.

## Follow-up
- Under 50 words, same thread.
- Add one new, useful detail (a link to relevant work, a sharper idea for their
  product) instead of just "bumping".
- No guilt-tripping.

## Always
- Use only facts given to you. If a fact is missing, leave it out rather than invent it.
