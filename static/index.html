FAQ

Questions people ask

How much does it cost?

Try everything free for 2 days. After that, from ₹10 a day: ₹100 for 7 days, or ₹300 for 30 days. That covers fresh startup matches, founder details, drafted emails and follow-up reminders.

Where do the startups come from?

Indian startup funding news from Inc42, Entrackr, YourStory and ET Startups, recent Y Combinator companies, and Bangalore Startup Map (a directory of Bangalore startups, used with permission), refreshed regularly. Open roles come from each startup's public Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby job board.

Which startups do you cover?

Right now, pre-seed and seed-stage startups based in Bangalore: small teams that just raised and are likely hiring their first people. More cities and later stages are coming.

How do you find founder emails?

From public sources: the startup's website, its public GitHub, and email lookup services. Every address is labelled with how sure we are, so you know when a draft might bounce.

Do you send emails for me?

No. We write the email and open it in your Gmail (or your email app) with everything filled in; you press send. We never get access to your inbox. When it's time to follow up, we remind you and write that one too.

Can I use my LinkedIn instead of a resume?

Yes. On LinkedIn, open your profile, click More (or Resources), then Save to PDF, and attach that file. Paste your LinkedIn profile link too: we add it to every email so founders can look you up.

Do I need to know which startups I want?

No. Share your resume and say what you're after, like "backend roles, fintech, remote", and we'll do the searching.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Deleting your account removes your profile, drafts and history.
